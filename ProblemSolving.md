# Problem Solving Principals in General

## Concrete examples

Manually solve concrete instances, then build a general solution out of it.

## Case analysis

Define different cases for your problem (e.g. Tower Breaker) and solve each case individually.

## Iterative Refinement

Many problems can be solved using brute-force. Try those and refince afterwards.

## Reduction 

Use simpler problems or known solutions as subroutines by leveraging existing solutions or breaking down large, complex problems into smaller managable pieces.
